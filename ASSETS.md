## Floating platter

`public/assets/platter-cutout.webp` was created with the built-in image-generation tool from the supplied 1.49.07 PM photo and compressed into WebP while preserving transparency. It is an AI-assisted extraction, and may have small detail differences from the original. The unedited source photo is also included as `platters.webp`.

Prompt used:

> Use case: background-extraction. Asset type: transparent cutout for the Nosh restaurant website. Edit target: supplied real restaurant food photograph. Isolate ONLY the upper, fully visible grey oval platter containing four golden breaded patties with white sauce drizzles, the attached yellow sauce bowl, and its existing garnish. Remove the lower duplicate platter, the tabletop and all surrounding background. Preserve the upper platter's exact shape, grey ceramic texture, four patties, toppings, sauce, colours and photographic realism. Do not redesign the food or add text or decorations. Center this single complete platter on a genuinely transparent background with generous margin so all edges remain visible. Landscape image, no cropping of the selected platter.
